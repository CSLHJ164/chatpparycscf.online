What is Grow a Garden?

Grow a Garden is a wildly popular farming simulation game on Roblox, where players plant seeds, grow crops, and harvest fruits to earn Sheckles, the in-game currency. Understanding Mutations

Mutations are special modifiers that dramatically increase the value of your crops in Grow a Garden. They occur through various weather events, special conditions, and random chances while your plants grow.

Growth Mutations

Affected by watering patterns and soil conditions. Wet (5x), Dry (3x), and Overwatered (7x) mutations occur based on how you care for your plants.

Temperature Mutations

Triggered by weather events. Frozen (10x), Hot (8x), and Cold (6x) mutations happen during specific weather conditions in the game.

Environmental Mutations

Rare and powerful mutations like Celestial (120x) and Shocked (100x) that occur during special events like Blood Moon or storm conditions.

Exclusive Rules

Some mutations can't be combined. For example, Golden and Rainbow are mutually exclusive - you can only have one per crop.
